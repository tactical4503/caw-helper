# CAW Helper Design Bible

## Identity
CAW Helper should feel like entering a wrestling creation laboratory and archive, not opening a SaaS dashboard.

## Core visual language
- black, warm paper, bone, muted red, steel
- large condensed display typography
- tactile lines and archival marks
- depth through scale, lighting, fog, shadows, and motion
- deliberate asymmetry
- dense but readable compositions

## Mask Maker
The mask world is a physical archive wall. Masks should feel positioned in space, not trapped in ordinary cards. The timeline is part of the environment.

## Interaction
Selected objects come forward. Background information dims. Inspection reveals history, materials, silhouette, construction, and lessons for CAW creators.

## Anti-patterns
No generic rounded SaaS cards, no random neon cyberpunk, no empty hero sections, no fake 3D, no decorative controls without purpose.
