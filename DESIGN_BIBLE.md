# CAW Helper Design Bible

## Identity
CAW Helper should feel like entering a wrestling creation laboratory and archive, not opening a SaaS dashboard.

## Core visual language
- black, warm paper, bone, muted red, steel and restrained gold
- large condensed display typography
- tactile lines and archival marks
- depth through scale, lighting, fog, shadows and motion
- deliberate asymmetry
- dense but readable compositions

## Physical-object rule
The primary visual object must be the object itself. A mask is a wearable wrestling mask, not a logo or symbolic face graphic. It should communicate volume, construction, material, seams/panels, eye openings, shadows and believable proportions.

## Mask Maker
The mask world is a physical archive wall. Masks should feel positioned in space, not trapped in ordinary cards. The timeline is part of the environment. The initial collection contains distinct original physical-object studies stored locally in `assets/objects/masks/`.

## Interaction
Selected objects come forward. Background information dims. Inspection reveals materials, silhouette, construction and lessons for CAW creators.

## Anti-patterns
No generic rounded SaaS cards, no random neon cyberpunk, no empty hero sections, no fake 3D, no decorative controls without purpose, no placeholder object art.
